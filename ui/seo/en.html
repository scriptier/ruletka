How it works

Press Start, allow the camera when the browser asks, and you are in a video chat. There is no email, no phone number, and no password for the basic loop. If the person is not a match, go to the next stranger. If you want to talk again, add them with a friend code and call later when you are both online.

Video goes browser to browser (peer-to-peer). Ruletka.me does not record the conversation and does not upload the live video to a server you watch it from. Signaling for matchmaking is separate from the media. A partner can still point a phone at their screen, so the product includes Block, Report, and blur.

What you can do

  • Start a random video chat with no sign-up.
  • Keep a friend with a code and place a video call later.
  • Block or report someone in the chat. Blur is there if you want a second before you look.
  • Use the site on a phone or a computer. Android also has an app.

The service is strictly 18+. The first screen asks you to confirm your age and the rules, then one Start button opens the camera. Safety notes live on the safety page (Russian).

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and press Start. Mail and a phone number are not required.

Where does the video go?

It is peer-to-peer between the two browsers. We do not record it. Use Block and Report if a partner records or breaks the rules.

Is it 18+?

Yes. You confirm you are 18 or older before the camera starts.
