Ruletka.me · Omegle alternative

A free Omegle alternative with peer-to-peer video

Omegle closed in November 2023. Ruletka.me is a free random video chat in the browser: no account, peer-to-peer video, friend codes, and block and report. 18+ only.

Start chatting →

What is different

You do not create a login to meet a stranger. Press Start, allow the camera, and the next person is a random adult who did the same. Video stays between the two browsers. We do not sell a “top users” board and we do not ask you to pay to skip.

If a chat is good, share a friend code and call that person later. If it is not, use Next. If it breaks the rules, use Block and Report. On-device checks can blur and skip clear violations. That is not a promise that every frame is reviewed by a person.
