Video call with no sign-up — a friend, or someone random

Call a friend on video for free, with no account: exchange a friend code and call from the browser or Android. Video is peer-to-peer. Or start a random video call. 18+.

Start chatting →

This service is 18+ only. It is for a call with an adult friend, or for random adult video chat. It is not for calling children, students, or a family member who is under 18. If you need an ordinary family call, use another product. Here you confirm you are 18 before the camera starts.

Three steps to call a friend

  1. Open Ruletka.me and press Start chatting. On one screen, confirm you are 18 and allow the camera and microphone. Your friend code is in the friends section.
  2. Send the code to the person you want to call. They open the same site, or the Android app, enter the code, and accept. There is no call until both of you confirm the friendship.
  3. When you are both online, press Call. They accept. Video connects directly between the devices. No account, email, or phone number.

No app required

The call works in Chrome, Yandex Browser, Firefox, and Safari. Nothing to install on a computer. Android has an app in RuStore and Google Play if you want an icon. There is no separate iPhone app: open the site in Safari.

A random video call is the roulette: Start chatting looks for another adult who is free, not for a named friend. How that chat works is on the Russian page video chat with no sign-up. The English home is here.

Where the video goes

The call is peer-to-peer. We do not store a recording to watch later. A report can include one still. The server only helps the two devices find each other and exchange WebRTC details. If the network cannot connect directly, media may pass through a TURN relay. That channel is encrypted, and we do not save a movie from it.

The other person can still screen-record on their side. Block and report are in the call. Rules are on the safety page.

Questions

How do I call a friend with no account?

Share a friend code, both accept, then Call when you are both online.

Do I need an app?

No. A browser is enough. Android is optional. On iPhone, use Safari.

Where does the video go?

Directly between the devices. We do not record it. A report can include one still.

Can I use this to call a child or a grandparent?

No. The service is strictly 18+.
