What anonymous means here

There is no sign-up. No legal name, email, password, or phone number is required. An identifier is created in this browser so the site can remember friends and avoid someone you blocked. A display name is optional. To the other person you are the camera, not a profile.

That is not the same as being impossible to identify. A direct WebRTC call connects two devices. In that connection your network address (IP) can be visible to the other participant. Our server also sees the connection address: we use it for country and city unless you hide location, and we use it when a report leads to a restriction. Your provider can see that you opened the site. Covering your face, voice, or room is up to you.

We do not store the video

Picture and sound go peer-to-peer and are not recorded on our side. If a direct path fails, media may use a TURN relay. The channel is encrypted. We do not keep a recording from it. A report can include one frame that the reporter chose to attach. That is not a recording of the whole call.

The other person can record their screen. We do not control that. Blur the frame, block them, and send a report. Details are on the safety page.

How to share less

  • Do not give a surname, address, school, or workplace, and do not show documents, tickets, or a banking screen.
  • Do not follow “send a photo” links, and do not send money.
  • Hide IP in settings routes media through a relay and hides country and city from the partner. It does not erase you from the internet.
  • Cover the camera when you are not in a call. Anyone in the room is in the frame with you.

The service is strictly 18+. A call to a specific person is described on free video call.

Whether the page opens depends on your provider. We do not promise access on every network. If the page opened, the chat in it works.

Questions

What does anonymous mean here?

No account and no phone number. A direct WebRTC connection can still reveal an IP.

Do you record the video?

No. We do not store the live video. A partner can still screen-record.

Can I hide my country and IP from the partner?

Yes, with Hide IP. That is a media relay and a hidden country, not full invisibility.

Will the site open on every network?

No. It depends on the network and the provider.
