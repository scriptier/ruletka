2. What the Service is

ruletka.vip provides random and friends-based video chat in the browser. Typical pieces:

  • Matchmaking & signaling — a hub (bridge) helps pair users and exchange WebRTC offers/answers/ICE and text chat for the session.
  • Video & audio — when the network allows, media is peer-to-peer WebRTC (encrypted on the media path). We do not run a permanent video recording store for calls.
  • TURN / relay — if a direct path fails, encrypted media may pass through a relay. Quality and availability depend on your network and capacity.
  • Optional features — rooms, friends, party browse, messages, device settings, themes, and similar tools as shown in the client.
