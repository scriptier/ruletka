Community guidelines

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Be 18+

Only adults. No minors. Ever. Report underage concerns immediately — a single report can restrict matching while operators review logs.

Be decent

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, or doxxing.
  • No illegal content.
  • If someone is inappropriate, use Next, Stop, Block, or Report.

Friends

Friend requests require Accept. Do not spam codes or calls.

Safety tools

Blur-first, on-device AI skip, and multi-report auto-restricts help when there is no full-time moderator. Details: Safety.

Seeders / operators

If you run a federated hub, keep abuse controls and do not share federation tokens publicly.
