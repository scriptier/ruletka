Request data deletion

Last updated: 2026-08-05

ruletka does not use a classic email/password account. Identity and most chat history live on your device (browser site data or the official app me.ruletka.app). Some friend, block, report, and stars records may be stored on the hub you connect to (for example ruletka.vip / ruletka.me).

1. Delete data on your device (immediate)

  1. Open the app or live chat.
  2. Go to Settings → clear / reset local data (or clear site data in your browser for this site).
  3. Optionally uninstall the app or clear the browser profile for this site.

That removes local identity, preferences, and match chat history stored on that device.

2. Request hub-side deletion

To request review or deletion of server-side records (friends, blocks, reports, or related logs) on the seed hub, email:

privacy@ruletka.me

Please include when possible:

  • Your friend code (if you still have it)
  • Approximate dates you used the service
  • Whether you used the website, Android app, or both

We will process legitimate deletion requests as described in our Privacy policy — Retention and deletion. We may not be able to erase every operational log, data already received by a chat partner, or records required for safety/enforcement for a limited period.

3. Other hubs

If you connected to a third-party self-hosted hub, that operator controls their server data. Contact that hub’s operator for deletion there, in addition to clearing your device.
