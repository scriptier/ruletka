Connection problem

Video couldn’t connect cleanly. Try the tips below — most issues are Wi‑Fi, permissions, or a strict network.

  • Use Wi‑Fi if possible (some mobile networks block WebRTC)
  • Allow camera & mic for this site, then reload
  • Tap Next to find someone else (their network may be blocked)
  • VPN or corporate networks often break peer video — try without VPN
