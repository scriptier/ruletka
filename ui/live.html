How stars work

Free reputation, not money. Earn from long chats, spend on gifts, unlock stronger reports at 100★ and 250★.

  • Earn: +1★ each after 1 hour. Talk to a senior (250+): normals get +3★ (15+ min), trusted get +2★ (1 hour). Gift once after 15+ min — up to 2★ at 100+, up to 3★ at 250+.
  • Spend: Heart 1★ · Bars/Flowers/Balloons/Confetti 5★ · Fireworks 15★ · Please stay 30★ (they can’t Next for 15s, once/month per person).
  • Trust: 100+★ reports ×2, 250+★ ×3. High-rep users are harder to ban — seniors cannot auto-ban each other.
  • Safe: stars stay on the server for your account — profile export never includes them.
