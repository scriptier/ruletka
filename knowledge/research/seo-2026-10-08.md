## 0. TL;DR
1. **The head term is «чат рулетка».** In Google Trends RU over 12 months its average interest is 80, against 24 for «видеочат» and 50 for «некто ми». «ome tv» scores 6, «чатрулетка» (one word) 3, and «омегл» about 0. Long tails such as «видеочат без регистрации», «анонимный видеочат», «аналог чат рулетки» and «случайный видеочат» each average about 0 to 0.3 on the same scale. They are real queries (they show in autocomplete) but small. Win them with modifiers on «чат рулетка» / «видеочат рулетка».
2. **Strongest modifiers by Trends "related/rising" and Yandex Suggest:** *видео* (видео чат рулетка, top related = 100), *скачать / на андроид / на айфон* (rising +140…+300%), *голосовая / голосовой режим* (Yandex suggest dominated by it; Trends 24 against 66 for видео чат рулетка), *онлайн*, *некто ми* (competitor brand), *с камерой* (+250%), *без регистрации*, and troubleshooting (*не видит камеру и микрофон*, *почему не грузит*).
3. **Ruletka's biggest problems are on-page.** The index is a meta-refresh/JS redirect to live.html. Server-side domain rewriting produces "ruletka.me and ruletka.me" in titles/descriptions on ruletka.me. home.html is `lang="ru"` but serves about 257 words of English text (6 Cyrillic words). The H1 is "Meet someone new. Face to face.", which contains no target keyword. hreflang points at `/?lang=xx` URLs that redirect and canonicalize to /live.html. live.html's only H1 is the age gate "Are you 18 or older?".
4. **Brand collision.** **ruletka.co** ("Чат рулетка онлайн бесплатно - видеочат без регистрации | Ruletka") ranked #1 in a web search for «чат рулетка». ruletka.chat, camruletka.com and the Google Play app "Чат Рулетка" (com.chat.ruletka) also use the name. A brand query like "ruletka.me видеочат" does return ruletka.me/home.html first, so it is indexed. Use the domain-style name «Ruletka.me» consistently.
5. **Competitors win with intent landing pages.** rusletka.ru has /besplatnyj-videochat, /anonimnyj-chat, /chat-s-devushkami, /russkij-videochat, /videochat-na-telefone and /alternativa-omegle. Nightcap has /ru/free-video-chat, /ru/random-video-call, /ru/talk-to-strangers, /ru/chat/{country}, /ru/interests/{x} and /ru/compare/nightcap-vs-{x}. Vidibro has /omegle-alternative, /coomeet-alternative, /video-chat-russia and similar. Most top results serve 400–2,400 words of server-rendered text, an FAQPage schema and a native-language H1 containing the keyword.

### 2c. Patterns
1. **Exact-match keyword in title AND H1, in Russian**: «Чат рулетка», «видеочат», «без регистрации», «бесплатно», «онлайн», «анонимный».
2. **One landing page per intent**: без регистрации / бесплатный, анонимный, на телефоне / для Android, русский видеочат, альтернатива Omegle / аналог чат рулетки, голосовой чат, знакомства / общение, по странам (/chat/de, /video-chat-russia), по интересам, compare (X vs Y), plus a blog ("лучшие видеочаты 2026", "безопасность").
3. **Path-based i18n** (/ru/, /en/, and so on) with full reciprocal hreflang. Nobody relies on `?lang=` and JS translation.
4. **Server-rendered text** of 400–2,400 words with how-it-works, features, safety and FAQ sections, plus **FAQPage** schema (and WebApplication/Organization).
5. **18+ and safety messaging** used as differentiation (Nightcap "только для 18+", LemonChat/Shomla AI moderation, SpinFacer "без фейков/ботов", Cafe "проверенные пользователи"). Ruletka's P2P, no-upload, block/report and on-device AI angle fits this well.
6. **Voice mode** is a big RU niche (nekto.me, Shomla, ruletka.co). Target it only if Ruletka supports audio-only or camera-off chat.
